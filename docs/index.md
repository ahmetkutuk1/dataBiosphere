Converts high-risk research findings (health, finance, regulation) into
auditable cinematic video and interactive simulation. Provides a
deterministic metaphor matrix (S1-S12 across fluvial, arboreal and
celestial families) and a truth-overlay generator that keeps literal
findings (effect size, 95% CI, n) visible in every frame, in line with
the Freeze-Frame Test and the Faithfulness Constraint.
